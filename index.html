Privacy Policy

Bible Journey: Merge & Story (com.born2play.biblejourney)
Last updated: 13 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you play Bible Journey: Merge & Story (the “Game”), how it is used, and the choices you have. The Game is published by born2play (“we”, “us”).

1. Information we collect

  • Game progress and settings — level, camp construction state, inventory, purchases of in-game currency, and preferences. Stored locally on your device.
  • Device and technical data — device model, operating system version, language, region, app version, and a resettable advertising identifier (Google Advertising ID on Android).
  • Usage data — gameplay events such as sessions, level completion, and ad views, used in aggregate to understand how the Game is played.
  • Purchase data — records of in-app purchases processed by Google Play. We do not receive your card number or full payment details.
  • Support correspondence — if you email us, we receive your email address and message.

We do not ask for your name, precise location, contacts, photos, or microphone access.

2. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded and interstitial ads through AppLovin MAX and its mediated ad networks. These partners may use your advertising identifier and device data to select and measure ads. Depending on your region, you may be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and you can limit ad personalisation there.

3. Analytics

We use aggregated, non-identifying analytics to measure stability and gameplay balance. Analytics data is not used to build advertising profiles.

4. How we use information

  • Operate the Game and keep your progress consistent across sessions;
  • Deliver, cap, and measure advertising;
  • Verify in-app purchases and prevent fraud;
  • Diagnose crashes and improve balance and performance;
  • Respond to support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Sharing

We share limited data with service providers acting on our behalf: Google Play (billing and distribution), AppLovin and mediated ad networks (advertising), and analytics providers. These providers are contractually restricted to using the data only to deliver their service.

6. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the Game or clear its data. Support emails are kept for up to 24 months. Aggregated analytics are retained in non-identifying form.

7. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, UK, or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your data, and to object to personalised advertising. To exercise these rights, email us at the address below. You can also delete all local data by uninstalling the Game.

9. Security

Data in transit is protected with HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we handle.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by the “Last updated” date above and, where appropriate, an in-Game notice.
